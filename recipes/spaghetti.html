Spaghetti and Meatballs

This recipe is an easy to make version of spaghetti and meatballs that I found online.

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti noodles
  • Meatballs
  • Tomato Sauce

How to make it!

  1. Boil the pasta

    2. Bring some water to a boil and throw the pasta into it. Cook until pasta is al dente.

  2. Cook the meatballs
  3. Mix the sauce, pasta, and meatballs together
  4. Adjust Consistency
